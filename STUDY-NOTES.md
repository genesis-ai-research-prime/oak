## What OAK is

OAK is the **Open Agentic Kernel**: an open execution contract and assurance profile — specification, reference kernel and conformance suite — drawn from Evan's paper *Admissibility Without Oracles* (Draft 2, September 2026; the work dates from March 2026). In the stack it is the open layer: the personal viewer per individual — the definition Evan himself gave it, in the Founder's words: *"I hadn't seen OAK as the personal viewer per individual before but once you see it you can't unsee it."* Each person looks through OAK at their own deployed agents.

The Founder's framing, given to Evan 15 November 2026: **Firefox for Agents** — the open, user-side client standing between people and platform-owned agent runtimes, stewarded openly, as Mozilla stewards Firefox. Evan's standing study task, from the same conversation: study Firefox's history and evolution, and model OAK on Mozilla's failures.
